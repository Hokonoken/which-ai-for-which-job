Which AI for which job: the six-part map

Six articles on choosing the kind of AI system a job calls for, sorted not by the name on the slide but by two questions: who decides, and who acts. Each part takes one family, places three generic jobs on it, names what the platform from the agent stack blueprint has to provide, and quotes the article of law that applies. Read in order; the map in part 1 is the reference for all the others.
